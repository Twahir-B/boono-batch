# Boono Batch

**Broiler batch record app for Android.** Track feed, mortality, weight, costs, sales and profit for each batch of chickens, on your phone and fully offline.

## What it does
- **Batch Setup:** farm, house/pen, supplier, placement date, number of chicks, chick cost, selling price, stocking density, and optional targets for final weight and FCR.
- **Feed Program:** feed phases and water requirements, with a hot-weather water ratio.
- **Daily Record:** log mortality, average weight and feed each day, with a day-by-day view.
- **Medication Log:** includes a standard vaccination schedule that you can add automatically when you set the placement date.
- **Operational Costs and Sales:** record expenses and sales lots, with totals worked out for you.
- **Batch Report:** mortality %, final FCR, cost per bird, net profit/loss, profit margin, break-even price and break-even birds, plus trend charts.
- **Share and export:** copy a summary for WhatsApp, print, or export to Excel or Word.
- **Backup:** download a backup file and restore it later. Save finished batches to a library and start a new one.
- Dark mode and desktop view. Amounts are in **UGX**.

## Download and install
1. Go to the [**Releases**](../../releases) page and download `boono-batch-1.2.2.apk`.
2. Open the file on your Android phone.
3. If asked, allow **Install unknown apps** for your browser or file manager.
4. Open **Boono Batch** and read the "Things you must understand before you start" screen.

**Requires:** Android 7.0 or newer.

## Good to know
- This is a **debug-signed build** for testing and sideloading. It isn't from the Play Store.
- Your records are stored on your device. Use **Download backup** regularly. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it will delete your batches.
- The app asks for storage and internet permissions. Storage is used for exports and backups.
