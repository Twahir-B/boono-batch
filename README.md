# B-records

**Poultry batch records for broiler and layer farmers.** Write down what happens in each batch — feed, deaths, weights, eggs, medicine, costs and sales — and see whether you are making money. One app, two kinds of batches. Works on your phone, fully offline.

Built by **Boono Innovz Team** · Amounts are in **UGX**.

### Install on Android
1. Open the [Releases](../../releases) page and download `B-records.apk`.
2. Open the downloaded file.
3. If Android asks, allow **Install unknown apps** for your browser or file manager.
4. Open **B-records** and read the short start screen.

Requires Android 7.0 or newer. The app is not on the Play Store, so Android may warn you the first time. That is normal for apps installed from a file.

Open the web link above. For an app-like icon:
- **iPhone (Safari):** Share → **Add to Home Screen**
- **Android (Chrome):** menu → **Install app**

After the first visit with internet, the web app also opens without internet.

## What it does

**For broiler batches**
- Batch setup, feed program and water needs (with a hot-weather ratio)
- Daily record: feed, deaths, average weight, temperature
- Medication and vaccination log
- Costs and sales per lot
- Batch report: mortality %, FCR, cost per bird, profit, break-even

**For layer batches**
- Choose what you bought: day-old chicks, young pullets or point-of-lay pullets
- Eggs, broken eggs and sizes entered daily; trays worked out for you
- Hen-day % and hen-housed %, weekly summary, phases and feed
- Vaccine schedule and health log
- Break-even in plain words: how many trays you must sell to cover your costs

**For both**
- **Today** screen: type what happened today, see your batch so far, and share the day by WhatsApp or other apps
- Keep many batches in your library and open any of them later
- Print, or export to Excel or Word
- Undo and redo, dark mode, desktop view
- Number checks: no negative amounts, no more deaths than birds placed, no more broken eggs than eggs collected

## Workers and managers

A manager can let a worker enter the daily numbers **without seeing any prices or profit**.

1. **Manager:** menu ☰ → **Invite a worker (starter)**. Send the message by WhatsApp or save it as a file.
2. **Worker:** open B-records → **I'm a worker** → paste the message (or open the file) and type their name.
3. **Worker:** each day, enter feed, deaths, eggs or weights in **Today**. Then menu ☰ → **Send my records**.
4. **Manager:** menu ☰ → **Merge worker records** → paste or open the worker's message.

When the manager merges, new figures are filled in, matching figures are ignored, and any difference is shown so the manager chooses which number to keep. Merging the same message twice changes nothing. Merged days are marked "entered by" the worker's name.

A worker cannot leave worker mode until their latest records have been sent.

## Your records and backups

- Your records are stored **on your device**, not on a server. Nobody else can see them.
- **Download a backup regularly.** Clearing the app's data, clearing the browser's data or uninstalling can delete your batches.
- The library password protects the saved batches in your library. It does not lock the batch on your screen.
- **Moving from the old Boono Batch or Boono Layer app?** B-records installs next to it and does not replace it. In the old app, use **Download backup**. In B-records, use menu ☰ → **Restore backup** and choose that file. Backups from the old apps are accepted.

## Good to know

- B-records does not replace advice from your veterinarian. Vaccine schedules and feed guides are common starting points, not instructions for your flock.
