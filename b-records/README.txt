Put B-records inside your existing repository (Twahir-B/boono-batch)
1. Unzip this file. You get a folder called b-records.
2. Open github.com/Twahir-B/boono-batch > "Add file" > "Upload files". On a computer, drag the whole b-records FOLDER in (this keeps it separate from anything already in the repository). Press "Commit changes".
3. Open Settings > Pages. Source: "Deploy from a branch", Branch: main, folder: / (root). Save. (If Pages is already on, skip this.)
4. After about a minute the app is at https://twahir-b.github.io/boono-batch/b-records/
5. Android Chrome: menu > "Install app". iPhone Safari: Share > "Add to Home Screen".
To update later: upload the new index.html into the same b-records folder.
